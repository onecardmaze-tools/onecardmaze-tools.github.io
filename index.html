    One Card Maze · Infinite Walker

    Upload the front and back of a maze card. The walker starts anywhere you click and roams freely. The card you're standing on is held in the middle of the screen with the eight cards around it: the other side above, below, left and right, and the same side turned 180° in the four corners — the two faces behave as one map, so you keep walking instead of turning the card over. Step over an edge and the view slides the next card into the middle.

    Everything runs in your browser. Your images are saved locally on this device and never uploaded anywhere.

    How to play

    • Set your start — click / tap anywhere on the card once.
    • Walk — WASD or arrow keys.
    • Go somewhere — click / tap a spot and the walker walks there.
    • Cross any edge — all eight cards around you are drawn, so you can see where every edge leads before you get there. Left, right, up and down show the other side; the four corners show the same side turned 180° (a diagonal step crosses two folds). Step over an edge and the view slides the card you land on into the middle; keep going and you're simply on the other face.
    • Watch your "up" — crossing a side edge keeps you the same way up, but crossing a top or bottom edge tumbles the card, so you come out turned around 180°.
    • Rotate the card — Q / E or the ⟲ / ⟳ buttons rotate the whole card freely in 90° steps.
    • Zoom — mouse wheel / pinch, or the + / − buttons.
    • Pan — drag anywhere to look around; moving again brings your card back to the middle.
    • Markers — choose any shape, colour, and optional label, then click the map to place it. Markers repeat on every copy of that face and stay independent of walking, rotation, reset, and replay. Select a marker in the list to edit or remove it. There are no required starts, destinations, or routes.
    • Trail — solid ink records where you walked on every copy of a face. The outlined arrow stamps show which way was up at that visit, not your walking direction. They stay fixed to the card as you continue walking or zoom. An arrow pointing toward the top of your screen means you visited in the current orientation. Several arrowheads in one stamp record several orientations at that place. Small open chevrons › on the line show the original walking direction; opposing tips mean the corridor was walked both ways.
    • Undo & replay — once you've walked a little a transport bar appears at the bottom. ↶ steps back (hold it, or Z, to wind smoothly), ◀ / ▶ replay your whole walk backwards or forwards (Space / Shift+Space), the speed button cycles how fast that playback runs (0.1× / 0.2× / 0.5× / 1× / 2× / 4× — it starts slow, at 0.2×), and the slider scrubs anywhere along it. |‹ / ›| jump between flips and spins. The event menu jumps directly to a numbered event. Flip and spin events pause before and after the transition; the current face and up direction appear in the replay panel. The replay turns the card too, so you see the spins you made — and spins you made without walking collapse to the shortest single turn (three to the left replay as one to the right). Trail colours record the card’s up direction when drawn: gold ↑, blue →, red ↓, violet ←. They do not mean front/back or playback progress. Winding back is a preview: the complete route stays faintly visible and a white segment highlights the current visit, and the moment you walk again the part you wound past is dropped.
    • Reset — R clears the walker and lets you pick a new start.

    WebGL needed

    This app draws the card with a GPU shader. Your browser or device has WebGL turned off.
