Markers

Place anything you want to remember. Each marker repeats on every copy of its card face. Markers never move the walker.

Choose a style, then place it on the map.

    Recording

    One file with both card images, your complete route, spins, markers and current view. Sharing a recording also shares the card images it contains.

    Import replaces the current session; export first if you want to keep it. Files stay on your device until you choose to share them.

        Unofficial OneCardMaze helper

        One Card Maze is a maze game played by rotating and flipping a single card.

        This helper is fan-made. It isn't an official website, and it doesn't include any official mazes.

        Why I made this

        I've loved this game since its early days. But after making my own physical cards, I kept losing track of where I was and where I'd already been. So I made this little tool for myself, and for anyone else who has the same problem.

        I deliberately haven't included any of the game's mazes. Please get those from the creators. The demo here is just a simple practice card I made for trying out the tool.
