One Card Maze · Infinite Walker

Upload the front and back of a maze card. The walker starts anywhere you click and roams freely. The card you're standing on is held in the middle of the screen with the eight cards around it: the other side above, below, left and right, and the same side turned 180° in the four corners — the two faces behave as one map, so you keep walking instead of turning the card over. Step over an edge and the view slides the next card into the middle.

Everything runs in your browser. Your images are saved locally on this device and never uploaded anywhere.

How to play

  • Set your start — click / tap anywhere on the card once.
  • Walk — WASD or arrow keys.
  • Go somewhere — click / tap a spot and the walker walks there.
  • Cross any edge — all eight cards around you are drawn, so you can see where every edge leads before you get there. Left, right, up and down show the other side; the four corners show the same side turned 180° (a diagonal step crosses two folds). Step over an edge and the view slides the card you land on into the middle; keep going and you're simply on the other face.
  • Watch your "up" — crossing a side edge keeps you the same way up, but crossing a top or bottom edge tumbles the card, so you come out turned around 180°.
  • Rotate the card — Q / E or the ⟲ / ⟳ buttons spin the whole card 90°, so a door's arrow can point up.
  • Zoom — mouse wheel / pinch, or the + / − buttons.
  • Pan — drag anywhere to look around; moving again brings your card back to the middle.
  • Trail — toggles the path you've walked. It's a hollow ribbon whose colour records the card orientation it was laid at, with little arrows inside pointing to the card's "up" back then. It's marked on the card, so it shows on every copy of a face — walk off and come back and it's still there. Where a crossing tumbled you 180° a ⟲ badge is stamped beside the trail; a plain seam with no badge means you came out the same way up.
  • Undo & replay — once you've walked a little a transport bar appears at the bottom. ↶ steps back (hold it, or Z, to wind smoothly), ◀ / ▶ replay your whole walk backwards or forwards (Space / Shift+Space), the speed button cycles how fast that playback runs (0.1× / 0.2× / 0.5× / 1× / 2× / 4× — it starts slow, at 0.2×), and the slider scrubs anywhere along it. The replay turns the card too, so you see the spins you made — and spins you made without walking collapse to the shortest single turn (three to the left replay as one to the right). Winding back is a preview: the trace hides itself and the marker walks back along your route, and the moment you walk again the part you wound past is dropped.
  • Reset — R clears the walker and lets you pick a new start.

WebGL needed

This app draws the card with a GPU shader. Your browser or device has WebGL turned off.
